# TuneVision Support (Android XR)

**Welcome to TuneVision Support.** We are here to help you get the most out of your musical playground on Android XR.

## Supported Devices

Android XR headsets, such as the Samsung Galaxy XR.

TuneVision opens in a window, beside your other apps. You can also show it larger, on its own, in Full Space (see Settings below). Play standing or seated.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Getting Started

**How do I play?**
Look at a key and pinch, or point at it with your hand and pinch. Keep pinching and slide across the keys to run up and down the scale. Every key plays the note written on it, from DO to DO.

**How do I change instrument?**
Pick one in the instrument bar along the top of the window. When there are more instruments than the window is wide, slide the bar sideways. A velvet curtain closes and opens on the new instrument.

**Can I make the window bigger?**
Yes. Drag a corner of the window to resize it, or move it by its bar, as with any app on your headset. TuneVision fills the window at any size. For the largest view, open **Settings → Window → Full Space**; **Back to Home Space** returns it to a window beside your other apps.

### Instruments

**Which instruments are there?**
Twelve: Piano, Xylophone, Drums, Flute, Guitar, Saxophone, Harp, Pan Flute, and the Cat, Wolf, Duck and Cow Pianos. Each key on the piano, xylophone, flute, harp and saxophone has a Note Buddy that sings its note, and the same buddy always sings the same note.

**Why did the buddies close their eyes?**
After 30 seconds without a note, the band dozes off. Play any key to wake them up.

### Free Instruments and Purchases

**What is free?**
TuneVision is free to download. Piano, Xylophone and Drums are free, with no limits, no timers and no ads.

**What are the free tries?**
Each of the other nine instruments can be opened 10 times for free. The number on its icon in the instrument bar shows the tries left; a padlock means they are used up.

**How do I unlock an instrument?**
When you pick an instrument whose free tries are used up, the Instrument Store opens. Each instrument is a one-time purchase, or the Full Orchestra Bundle unlocks every instrument, including every new instrument we add in future updates. Purchases go through Google Play's own purchase screen, with Google's parental controls.

**My purchase is missing.**
Make sure the headset is online and signed in to the Google account that made the purchase, then reopen TuneVision: it asks Google Play for your purchases each time it opens. In the Instrument Store, **Restore Purchases** asks again. If it is still missing, contact us (see below).

### Stickers

**How do I earn stickers?**
Just by playing. There are 12:
- Play a Note Buddy's note 10 times, on any instrument, to earn that buddy's sticker (8 buddies, from Dottie on DO to Duke on the high DO).
- Play 3 different instruments to earn Sunny the Explorer.
- Play the Pan Flute to earn Teddy.
- Play lots of notes really fast to set off a party and earn Party Star.
- Come back and play on another day to earn Moonbeam.

Open the sticker book from the first button in the instrument bar. Stickers are free: nothing in the sticker book is for sale.

### Settings

**Where are the settings?**
The last button in the instrument bar. There you can switch between a window and Full Space, send us feedback, open this page and the privacy policy, and see the app's version and credits.

### Data and Privacy

**What data does TuneVision collect?**
None that we can see. Your stickers and free tries are stored on the device only. Purchases go through Google Play, and the app receives only the list of instruments you own. TuneVision has no servers, ads or analytics. For full details, including how to delete your data, see our [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY_ANDROID_XR.md).

### Troubleshooting

**There is no sound.**
Check the headset's volume. TuneVision plays alongside other apps' sound.

**Nothing happens when I pinch a key.**
Make sure you are looking or pointing at the TuneVision window, and pinch firmly.

**The App is crashing or not responding.**
1. Close the App and reopen it.
2. Make sure your headset is up to date.
3. Update TuneVision to the latest version from Google Play.
4. If the issue persists, contact us (see below).
