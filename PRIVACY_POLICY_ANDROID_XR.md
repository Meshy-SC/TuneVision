# TuneVision Privacy Policy — Android XR

- **App:** TuneVision (TuneVision: Musical Playground on Google Play)
- **Publisher:** Meshy (Ahmed Dalhi)
- **Last updated:** November 15, 2026

---

## Summary

TuneVision has no accounts, no servers, no advertising and no analytics. Your stickers and your free tries stay on your device. We cannot see them.

TuneVision is free to download and sells instruments through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment; the app only learns which instruments you own, on your device, so that it can unlock them.

Any user can ask us to delete their data at any time. Section 7 explains how.

## 2. Data the App Uses and How

### 2.1 Your TuneVision purchases (Google Play)

Piano, Xylophone and Drums are free. Each of the other nine instruments (Flute, Guitar, Saxophone, Harp, Pan Flute, and the Cat, Wolf, Duck and Cow Pianos) can be tried 10 times for free, then bought once. The Full Orchestra Bundle unlocks every instrument, including instruments added in later updates.

What happens:

- **Payment** happens entirely in Google Play's own purchase screen, with your Google account. We never see or receive your payment details, your name, your email address or your billing address.
- **What the app receives from Google Play, on your device:** the names and prices of these instruments in your language and currency, and the list of instruments your Google account bought in TuneVision, with a purchase reference for each one.
- **How it is used:** to open the instruments you own, to confirm each purchase to Google Play (as Google requires), and to restore your purchases after a reinstall or on another device: the app asks Google Play each time it opens, and **Restore Purchases** in the Instrument Store asks again. The app does not send this information anywhere else.

The app talks to Google Play through the Google Play Billing library, which is Google's code inside the app. Google handles purchases, and anything that library reports to Google, under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### 2.2 Your hands and the window

TuneVision runs in a window, beside your other apps or on its own in Full Space. Android XR turns your pinches and taps into touches on that window, and the app receives only where on its window you touch, to play the key there. It never receives camera images, information about your room, the shape of your hands, where your eyes look or where your head is, and it saves nothing about your touches.

### 2.3 Emails you send us

If you email us for support, through **Settings → Send Feedback** or otherwise, or to request deletion, we receive your email address, your message, and anything you include. The feedback email's subject line contains the app's version, the device model and its Android version, to help us answer. We use your email only to answer you or to handle your request. Your email passes through our email provider. We keep it only while we handle your request. For deletion requests, we delete your email once we have confirmed the deletion to you.

## 3. Data That Stays on Your Device

To keep your game going, the app saves the following in its private storage on your device. **It is not sent to us, and we cannot see it.**

- How many of the 10 free tries you have used on each instrument you don't own
- Your sticker book: the stickers you have earned and which of them are new, how many times you have played each note (up to 10, the number a sticker needs), which instruments you have played, and the dates (day, month, year) of up to 14 days on which you played, for the "come back another day" sticker
- The instruments your Google account owns: a copy of Google Play's answer, so your instruments also open offline

**Android backup.** If backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include this data in the backup Google keeps for you, so that it can be restored on another device. Google runs that backup under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 4. Data We Do Not Collect

We collect nothing. In particular, TuneVision does **not** collect:

- Your name, email address or phone number (unless you email us, as in section 2.3)
- Your payment details
- Hardware serial numbers or advertising identifiers
- Location, whether precise or approximate
- Usage analytics or telemetry
- Camera images, room data, hand, eye or head data, recordings or voice: TuneVision never uses the microphone

The app contains no analytics or crash-reporting code of ours. If the app crashes and you have agreed to share diagnostics with Google, Android may report the crash to Google, and Google Play Console then shows us technical details: the device model, the Android and app versions, and where in the code the crash happened.

## 5. Where Your Data Is Stored and Who Can Access It

- **Your purchases** are stored by **Google**, as part of your Google account, under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- TuneVision has **no servers, no database and no user accounts** of its own. We (the developer) do not receive your identity or your list of purchases. Google Play Console shows us order records for our instruments (an order number, the item, the price and the country) and the crash details described in section 4.
- We do not use any data processors, service providers or partners that can access this data.

## 7. Requesting Data Deletion

**All users, in every country, can ask us to delete their data at any time. It is free, and you do not have to give a reason.**

### Data on your device

You can delete it yourself, immediately: **uninstalling TuneVision**, or clearing its storage in the device's settings, permanently deletes your stickers, your free-try counts and the copy of your purchases from your device. Purchases stay on your Google account: if you install the app again, they come back the next time it opens online.

### Data held by Google

Your purchases are part of your Google account, and Google keeps them so that you can restore them. You can manage or delete the data Google holds about your account with Google's own tools, described in [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We hold no copy of this data.

### Ask us

Email **davinci.dalhi@gmail.com** with the subject **"Data deletion request"**. Within **30 days**, we delete any emails you have sent us, help you remove the data described above, and confirm the deletion to you by email.

We will not refuse a deletion request. No law requires us to keep any of this data.

## 9. Network Activity

The game itself plays offline and opens no network connection of its own. The app uses the network only through Google Play and the Google Play Billing library, for purchases: names and prices, Google Play's purchase screen, the list of your purchases, and confirming each purchase (section 2.1).

## 10. System Permissions

- **Google Play purchases (`com.android.vending.BILLING`) and Internet (`INTERNET`):** used only by the Google Play Billing library, to reach Google Play for purchases.
- **Network state and wake lock (`ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`, `WAKE_LOCK`):** declared by Android libraries the app is built with. The game does not use them.

TuneVision does not request access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts, your room, your head position, eye tracking, hand tracking, files outside its own storage, or any advertising identifier.

TuneVision contains **no advertising, no analytics and no tracking code**. We do not sell, rent, share or transfer your data to anyone. The only other party involved is Google, which runs Android XR, Google Play and Google Play's purchases as described in section 2.
