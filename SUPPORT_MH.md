# TuneVision Support (Meta Quest)

**Welcome to TuneVision Support.** We are here to help you get the most out of your musical playground on Meta Quest.

## Supported Devices

- **Meta Quest 2**
- **Meta Quest Pro**
- **Meta Quest 3**
- **Meta Quest 3S**

TuneVision plays in a window, beside your other apps, in your room or in a Meta Horizon environment. Play standing or seated, with Touch controllers or with your hands.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Getting Started

**How do I play?**
Point at a key and pull the trigger (controllers), or pinch (hands). Keep holding and slide across the keys to run up and down the scale. Every key plays the note written on it, from DO to DO.

**How do I change instrument?**
Pick one in the instrument bar along the top of the window. When there are more instruments than the window is wide, slide the bar sideways. A velvet curtain closes and opens on the new instrument.

**Can I make the window bigger?**
Yes. Drag a corner of the window to resize it, or use the window's bar to move it, as with any app on your headset. TuneVision fills the window at any size.

### Instruments

**Which instruments are there?**
Twelve: Piano, Xylophone, Drums, Flute, Guitar, Saxophone, Harp, Pan Flute, and the Cat, Wolf, Duck and Cow Pianos. Each key on the piano, xylophone, flute, harp and saxophone has a Note Buddy that sings its note, and the same buddy always sings the same note.

**Why did the buddies close their eyes?**
After 30 seconds without a note, the band dozes off. Play any key to wake them up.

### Free Instruments and Purchases

**What is free?**
TuneVision is free to download. Piano, Xylophone and Drums are free, with no limits, no timers and no ads.

**What are the free tries?**
Each of the other nine instruments can be opened 10 times for free. The number on its icon in the instrument bar shows the tries left; a padlock means they are used up.

**How do I unlock an instrument?**
When you pick an instrument whose free tries are used up, the Instrument Store opens. Each instrument is a one-time purchase, or the Full Orchestra Bundle unlocks every instrument, including every new instrument we add in future updates. Purchases go through the Meta Horizon Store's own checkout. If your Meta account is managed by a parent, Meta asks the parent to approve the purchase.

**My purchase is missing.**
Make sure the headset is online and signed in to the account that made the purchase, then restart TuneVision: it asks the Meta Horizon Store for your purchases each time it starts. In the Instrument Store, **Restore Purchases** asks again. If it is still missing, contact us (see below).

### Stickers

**How do I earn stickers?**
Just by playing. There are 12:
- Play a Note Buddy's note 10 times, on any instrument, to earn that buddy's sticker (8 buddies, from Dottie on DO to Duke on the high DO).
- Play 3 different instruments to earn Sunny the Explorer.
- Play the Pan Flute to earn Teddy.
- Play lots of notes really fast to set off a party and earn Party Star.
- Come back and play on another day to earn Moonbeam.

Open the sticker book from the first button in the instrument bar. Stickers are free: nothing in the sticker book is for sale.

### Settings

**Where are the settings?**
The last button in the instrument bar. There you can send us feedback, open this page and the privacy policy, and see the app's version and credits.

### Data and Privacy

**What data does TuneVision collect?**
Very little, and we never see it. Your stickers and free tries are stored on the headset only. Purchases go through the Meta Horizon Store: Meta uses your Meta account's user ID to know which instruments you own, and the app receives only that list. As Meta requires for apps used by players under 13, the app also asks Meta for your account's age group, and keeps nothing of the answer. TuneVision has no servers, ads or analytics. For full details, including how to delete your data, see our [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY_MH.md).

### Troubleshooting

**There is no sound.**
Check the headset's volume. TuneVision plays alongside other apps' sound.

**Nothing happens when I point at a key.**
Make sure the TuneVision window is the one you are pointing at, and pull the trigger fully or pinch firmly. If you play with your hands, check that hand tracking is turned on in the headset's settings.

**The App is crashing or not responding.**
1. Close the App and reopen it.
2. Make sure your headset is running the latest Horizon OS version.
3. Update TuneVision to the latest version from the Meta Horizon Store.
4. If the issue persists, contact us (see below).
